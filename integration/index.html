The central connection

\[ F'(x)=f(x) \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \int_a^b f(x)\,dx=F(b)-F(a) \]

Differentiation describes local change. Integration accumulates local change. The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus connects these two ideas.
