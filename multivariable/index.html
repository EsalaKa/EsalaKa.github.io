The central idea

\[ f(x,y) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \nabla f \]

Single-variable calculus studies change along one input direction. Multivariable calculus studies how a quantity changes when several independent variables are involved.
