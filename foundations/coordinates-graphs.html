Coordinates and Graphs

Coordinates locate points. Graphs show relationships between quantities.

The main idea

A graph turns a mathematical relationship into a geometric picture.

\[ (x,y) \]

The ordered pair \((x,y)\) gives the position of a point in the coordinate plane.

1. The coordinate plane

The horizontal axis is the \(x\)-axis and the vertical axis is the \(y\)-axis.

They meet at the origin:

\[ (0,0). \]

The \(x\)-coordinate tells us how far to move horizontally, and the \(y\)-coordinate tells us how far to move vertically.

2. Ordered pairs

The order of coordinates matters.

\[ (2,5) \]

means

\[ x=2,\qquad y=5. \]

But

\[ (5,2) \]

is a different point.

3. Positive and negative coordinates

Coordinates may be positive, negative or zero.

For example,

\[ (-3,2) \]

means move \(3\) units left and \(2\) units up from the origin.

4. What a graph represents

A graph represents all pairs of values that satisfy a mathematical relationship.

For example,

\[ y=2x+1. \]

If \(x=0\), then

\[ y=1, \]

so \((0,1)\) lies on the graph.

If \(x=2\), then

\[ y=5, \]

so \((2,5)\) also lies on the graph.

5. A graph is more than a picture

A graph shows how one quantity changes in relation to another.

For a straight line,

\[ y=mx+c, \]

\(m\) describes the rate at which \(y\) changes as \(x\) changes, while \(c\) gives the value of \(y\) when \(x=0\).

6. Gradient

The gradient measures vertical change relative to horizontal change.

\[ \text{gradient} = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}. \]

For a straight line, this ratio is constant.

For example, if \(y\) increases by \(6\) while \(x\) increases by \(3\),

\[ \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac63 = 2. \]

So the gradient is \(2\).

7. Curves

Not every relationship gives a straight line.

For example,

\[ y=x^2 \]

produces a curve.

On a curve, the rate of change generally varies from point to point.

8. Why graphs matter for calculus

Calculus studies change.

On a graph, change appears as slope. For a straight line, the slope is constant. For a curve, the slope changes.

The derivative will later give the slope of a curve at a particular point.
