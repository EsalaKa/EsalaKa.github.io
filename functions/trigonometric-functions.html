FUNCTIONS 10

Trigonometric Functions

Trigonometric functions describe relationships involving angles and repeating behaviour.

The main idea

The three basic trigonometric functions are

\[ \sin x, \qquad \cos x, \qquad \tan x. \]

They can be understood from right triangles and, more generally, from points moving around the unit circle.

1. Sine, cosine and tangent

For an angle \(\theta\) in a right triangle,

\[ \sin\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}, \] \[ \cos\theta = \frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}, \] \[ \tan\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}. \]

These ratios depend only on the angle, not on the size of the triangle.

2. The unit circle

For calculus, the unit-circle interpretation is more useful.

A point on the unit circle has coordinates

\[ (\cos\theta,\sin\theta). \]

So cosine gives the horizontal coordinate and sine gives the vertical coordinate.

3. Radians

Calculus normally measures angles in radians rather than degrees.

One full revolution is

\[ 2\pi \]

radians, so

\[ 180^\circ=\pi \]

radians.

For example,

\[ 90^\circ=\frac{\pi}{2}. \]

4. Periodic behaviour

Sine and cosine repeat after one full revolution.

\[ \sin(x+2\pi)=\sin x, \] \[ \cos(x+2\pi)=\cos x. \]

This repeating behaviour is called periodicity.

5. Values of sine and cosine

Some important values are

\[ \sin 0=0, \qquad \cos 0=1, \] \[ \sin\frac{\pi}{2}=1, \qquad \cos\frac{\pi}{2}=0, \] \[ \sin\pi=0, \qquad \cos\pi=-1. \]

6. Tangent

Tangent can be written in terms of sine and cosine:

\[ \tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}. \]

Therefore tangent is undefined whenever

\[ \cos x=0. \]

For example, it is undefined at

\[ x=\frac{\pi}{2}. \]

7. A fundamental identity

Because a point on the unit circle satisfies

\[ x^2+y^2=1, \]

and because

\[ x=\cos\theta, \qquad y=\sin\theta, \]

we obtain

\[ \sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1. \]

This identity is fundamental throughout trigonometry and calculus.

8. Transforming trigonometric functions

A function such as

\[ y=A\sin(Bx+C)+D \]

can change the amplitude, period and position of the basic sine curve.

The constant \(A\) changes the vertical size, while \(B\) changes how quickly the pattern repeats.

9. Why trigonometric functions matter for calculus

Trigonometric functions have simple and closely related derivatives.

\[ \frac{d}{dx}\sin x=\cos x, \] \[ \frac{d}{dx}\cos x=-\sin x. \]

They are therefore central in calculus, differential equations, waves, oscillations and periodic models.
