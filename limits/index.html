CHAPTER 03

Change and Limits

Limits are one of the central ideas of calculus. This chapter develops them in stages: first the basic meaning, then one-sided, algebraic, infinite and long-run limits, followed by continuity.

\[x\to a\quad\Longrightarrow\quad f(x)\to L\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=L\]
