FULL LEARNING PATH

From foundations to stochastic calculus

01

Mathematical Foundations

Number systems, algebra, equations, functions, graphs, mathematical change and notation.

Explore → 02

Functions and Elementary Models

Function notation, domain and range, linear, quadratic, exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions.

Explore → 03

Change and Limits

Finite change, average rate of change, secant lines, limits and continuity.

Explore → 04

Differentiation

Derivatives from first principles, \(dy/dx\), differentiation rules and applications.

Explore → 05

Integration

Accumulation, area, antiderivatives, definite integrals, the fundamental theorem and integration techniques.

Explore → 06

Differential Equations

First-order and second-order ODEs, initial conditions, systems, stability and mathematical models.

Explore → 07

Multivariable Calculus

Partial derivatives, gradients, multiple integrals, vector fields, divergence and curl.

Explore → 08

Calculus for Modelling

Rates, growth and decay, population models, epidemic models, equilibria and numerical methods.

Explore → 09

Probability Foundations

Probability, random variables, distributions, expectation, variance and conditional probability.

Explore → 10

Stochastic Processes

Markov chains, continuous-time processes, transition rates, generators and Brownian motion.

Explore → 11

Stochastic Calculus

Brownian increments, \(dt\), \(dW_t\), stochastic differential equations, Euler–Maruyama and Itô calculus.

One continuous mathematical story

\[ x \rightarrow f(x) \rightarrow \Delta x \rightarrow \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} \rightarrow \lim_{\Delta x\to0} \rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} \rightarrow \int f(x)\,dx \rightarrow \frac{dy}{dt} \rightarrow dX_t \]

The site is organised so that each stage prepares the mathematical ideas needed for the next.
