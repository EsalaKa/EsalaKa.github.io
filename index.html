Where this is leading

\[ x \rightarrow f(x) \rightarrow \Delta x \rightarrow \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} \rightarrow \lim_{\Delta x\to0} \rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} \]

These foundations are not separate from calculus. They provide the language needed to understand what a derivative, integral and differential equation actually mean.
