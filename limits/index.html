Where this is leading

\[ \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} \rightarrow \lim_{\Delta x\to0} \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} \rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} \]

A derivative is built from a rate of change over a finite interval, followed by a limiting process in which that interval becomes arbitrarily small.
